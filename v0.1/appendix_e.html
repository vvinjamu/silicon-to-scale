The Principal's Reading Discipline
A principal engineer reads papers differently than a student does. Skim the abstract and conclusion first — if the claim isn't directly relevant to a system you're building or evaluating, move on. When you DO read a paper fully, you should be able to answer: (1) What problem does this solve? (2) What is the key insight? (3) What does this change about how I design or evaluate systems? (4) What are the hidden assumptions? If you can't answer all four, read again.
