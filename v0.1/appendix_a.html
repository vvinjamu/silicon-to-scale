A.5 Key Capacity Planning Formulas ★

MODEL PARAMETER MEMORY: model_bytes = N_params × bytes_per_param LLaMA-3 70B BF16: 70×10⁹ × 2 = 140 GB LLaMA-3 70B FP8: 70×10⁹ × 1 = 70 GB LLaMA-3 70B INT4: 70×10⁹ × 0.5 = 35 GB LLaMA-3 405B BF16: 405×10⁹ × 2 = 810 GB (requires TP=8 on H100, or 5× MI300X) KV CACHE MEMORY (per request): KV_bytes = 2 × L × H_kv × d_head × S × bytes_per_elem LLaMA-3 70B (L=80, H_kv=8, d_head=128, S=4096, BF16): = 2 × 80 × 8 × 128 × 4096 × 2 = 5.37 GB per concurrent request Per token per layer: 2 × H_kv × d_head × 2 = 2×8×128×2 = 4 KB TPOT MINIMUM (bandwidth-limited, batch=1): TPOT_min = model_bytes / HBM_bandwidth H100 BF16: 140 GB / 3.35 TB/s = 41.8 ms ★ H200 BF16: 140 GB / 4.8 TB/s = 29.2 ms B200 BF16: 140 GB / 8.0 TB/s = 17.5 ms MI300X BF16: 140 GB / 5.3 TB/s = 26.4 ms H100 INT4: 35 GB / 3.35 TB/s = 10.4 ms (4× speedup from quantization) PREFILL FLOPS (rule of thumb): FLOPs_prefill ≈ 2 × N_params × S (sequence length) LLaMA-3 70B, S=2048: 2 × 70×10⁹ × 2048 = 286 TFLOP On H100 (989 TFLOPS, 50% MFU): 286 / (989 × 0.5) = 578 ms MFU: MFU = (FLOPs_per_step × steps/s) / Peak_TFLOPS World-class: >55% | Good: 40–55% | Poor: <25% | Critical: <10% ALLREDUCE TIME (ring algorithm): t = 2 × (N−1)/N × M / BW [M = message bytes, N = GPUs, BW = link BW] TP=8, M=256 MB, NVLink=900 GB/s: t = 2×(7/8)×256e6/900e9 = 0.50 ms FLEET SIZING: raw_GPUs = peak_tok/s / (1000/TPOT_ms × batch_at_SLA) total_GPUs = raw_GPUs / 0.75 × 1.20 (75% util target, 20% burst headroom)

H200 insight: H200 has a LOWER ridge (206) than H100 (295) despite identical TFLOPS — because HBM bandwidth increased without adding compute. Ops that are just barely compute-bound on H100 may become memory-bound on H200. Re-classify every kernel's regime at each GPU generation transition.
